# [Personal] Artisan's Whisper: Newsletter Platform Comparison 📧

Jae, once you approve the draft, we need a place to send it. Here are the top 3 options for the Korean market, tailored for your solo-entrepreneur workflow.

## 1. Stibee (스티비) - The Industry Standard
- **Pros:** Best UI/UX in Korea. Extremely clean templates. Great deliverability.
- **Cons:** Paid tiers start early. 
- **Best for:** Professional look and brand building.
- **URL:** https://stibee.com/

## 2. Maily (메일리) - The Solo Creator's Choice
- **Pros:** Integrated monetization (subscriptions). Easy to set up. Growing community.
- **Cons:** Customization is slightly more limited than Stibee.
- **Best for:** Building a paid community or simple curation.
- **URL:** https://maily.so/

## 3. Substack - The Global Powerhouse
- **Pros:** Completely free to start. Massive discovery network. High trust.
- **Cons:** No native Korean localization (UI is English). Might look less "local" to some Korean users.
- **Best for:** Long-term scalability and international reach.
- **URL:** https://substack.com/
