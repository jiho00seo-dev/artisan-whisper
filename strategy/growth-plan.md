# [Personal] Artisan's Whisper: 0 to 100 Growth Plan 🚀

Jae, once the newsletter is live, the next challenge is getting eyes on it. Here is a high-impact, low-effort growth plan to get your first 100 subscribers.

## 📸 1. The "Aesthetic Seed" (Instagram/Threads)
- **Goal:** Drive curiosity through visual storytelling.
- **Action:** Post the curated "deskterior" items as a carousel.
- **Hook:** "Your desk is where you spend 8 hours a day. Why does it feel like a hospital room? 🌿"
- **CTA:** "Link in bio for the full list of artisanal finds."

## 🐦 2. The "Maker Shoutout" (X/LinkedIn)
- **Goal:** Leverage the audience of the artisans you feature.
- **Action:** Tag the brands/makers you featured in Issue #1.
- **Hook:** "Absolutely blown away by the craftsmanship of [Maker Name]. Featured them in the first issue of 'Artisan's Whisper' today."
- **Benefit:** Makers often retweet or share content that features them, exposing you to their followers.

## 🏢 3. The "Office Community" Play (Blind/Reddit)
- **Goal:** Capture the 'deskterior' enthusiasts.
- **Action:** Share a "My Desk Setup" post in workplace communities.
- **Hook:** "Finally finished my minimalist desk setup. The [Item Name] from Issue #1 was the missing piece."
- **CTA:** "I curate more items like this here: [Link]"

## 💡 Chunjae's Pro Tip:
"Don't just share a link. Share a **story** about why one specific item changed your day. People subscribe to people, not just catalogs."
