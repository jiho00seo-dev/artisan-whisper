# [Personal] Artisan's Whisper: Maker Outreach Etiquette ✉️

Jae, once you launch Issue #1, the next "Premium" step is reaching out to the makers you featured. This transforms you from a "Reviewer" into a "Partner."

## 🤝 Why reach out?
- **Exclusive Content:** They might send you high-res "Behind the scenes" photos for future issues.
- **Affiliate/Promo Codes:** You can negotiate unique discount codes (e.g., WHISPER10) for your subscribers.
- **Community Growth:** If a maker reshares your newsletter on their social media, you get instant high-quality traffic.

## ✍️ The "Maker-First" Template:

> **Subject:** We featured your work in Artisan's Whisper 🕯️
> 
> Hi [Maker Name],
>
> I’m Jae, the founder of **Artisan’s Whisper**—a curated newsletter dedicated to uncovering artisanal mastery for a high-intent audience.
>
> I wanted to let you know that we featured your **[Product Name]** in our debut issue. We chose it because [1-sentence genuine compliment about their craft].
>
> You can see the feature here: [Newsletter Link]
>
> We don't ask for anything in return—we just love what you do. However, if you have any high-res workshop photos you'd like us to share with our community in the future, we'd love to see them.
>
> Keep creating magic,
> Jae | Artisan's Whisper
