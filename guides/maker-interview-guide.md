# [Personal] Artisan's Whisper: Maker Interview Guide 🎙️

When you reach out to a maker to feature them in the "Whisper," your goal is to extract the **human story** that mass-market products lack. Here are the 5 questions that get the best answers.

## 🏹 The Core Questions

### 1. "The Obsession"
> *"What is the one detail about your process that most people never notice, but you refuse to compromise on?"*
- **Goal:** Finds the story of quality and integrity.

### 2. "The Texture"
> *"How do you want someone to feel the very first time their hands touch this item?"*
- **Goal:** Extracts tactile descriptions for your copy.

### 3. "The Origin"
> *"Is there a specific moment or a place that inspired the very first prototype of this piece?"*
- **Goal:** Adds geographical and emotional depth to the curation.

### 4. "The Longevity"
> *"How do you imagine this item looking or being used 10 years from now?"*
- **Goal:** Reinforces the "Artisan" value of timelessness.

### 5. "The Recommendation"
> *"Who is another artisan you deeply admire right now?"*
- **Goal:** Infinite scouting loop. Premium makers know other premium makers.
