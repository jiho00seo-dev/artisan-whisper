# [Personal] Artisan's Whisper: Visual Storytelling Guide 📸

Jae, since we are aiming for a "Premium" vibe, the visuals are as important as the words. Here is a 3-step guide to maintaining the 'Artisan' aesthetic on social media (Threads/Instagram).

## 🕯️ 1. The "Wabi-Sabi" Lighting
- **Rule:** Avoid harsh overhead lights or flashes. 
- **Tip:** Use side-lighting from a window during the "Golden Hour" (late afternoon). It creates soft shadows that highlight the texture of wood, concrete, or paper.
- **Vibe:** "Intimate Discovery" rather than "Retail Product."

## 🎞️ 2. The "Context" Shot (Macro + Environment)
- **Macro:** One close-up shot showing the maker's mark or a specific detail (e.g., the brass core of the HMM pen).
- **Environment:** One shot of the item in a "lived-in" space. Not a sterile studio, but a desk with a slightly messy open notebook or a real coffee cup nearby.
- **Vibe:** "This belongs in your life."

## ✍️ 3. The "Process" Teaser
- When featuring a maker, always look for a photo of their hands working or their workshop.
- **Why:** It justifies the premium price. People aren't paying for the object; they are paying for the 20 years of skill required to make it.
